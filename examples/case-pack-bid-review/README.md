# Case-pack review for distributor quotes

[Download the editable Excel worksheet](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hunterrosskonopasek-crypto/catalog-change-review/main/examples/case-pack-bid-review/case-pack-bid-review.xlsx)

A distributor can use this worksheet when a bid asks for a case price but lists demand in pieces. It converts a buyer’s estimate to exact case equivalents and whole cases for fulfillment. Enter a case price to compare the two extensions on one line. Prices stay in the copy you download. No account, macros or upload is required.

## Two lines from a published bid

| Source line | Calculation | Estimator’s decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cellulose sponges, p. 2 | 3,200 pieces ÷ 24 per case = 133⅓ case equivalents. Shipping 134 full cases would supply 3,216 pieces. | Which quantity should extend the quoted case price? Check the issuer’s instructions or relevant addendum before pricing. |
| Bowl clips, p. 3 | 12 clips per box × 6 boxes per case × 125 cases = 9,000 clips. | Use the supplier’s matching case price for the 125-case estimate. |

The workbook includes three more source lines. The soap refill has a case estimate and no pack count in that row, so the each quantity stays blank until the estimator supplies an approved pack reference.

## Use your own quantities

Enter the estimate unit (`case` or `each`), estimated quantity and the two packing factors in blue. Add a supplier case price when you want to compare extensions. In the lower panel, select the worksheet row to compare its exact and whole-case amounts. Each amount represents a scenario. The workbook leaves the pricing decision with the bidder.

Source: [Parish of Caddo IFB 26-18, Janitorial Supplies, Trash Liners, Paper Products, & Disposable Goods](https://caddo.gov/event/ifb-26-18-janitorial-supplies-trash-liners-paper-products-disposable-goods/), bid form pp. 2–3.
